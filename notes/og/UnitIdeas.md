# Unit Ideas

## Dwarves

- **Axeman**
- **Dwarven Craftsman:** not effective in combat; boosts attack (or possibly defense) for a limited duration.
- **Dwarven Twin Lords:** two strong units that are at their best near each other.
  - Possibly deal damage in a line between them.
  - If close enough, they can jump to each other, or toss each other.
  - If using the dwarves' defense-over-time mechanic and the twins are right next to each other, they become almost invincible.
- **Dwarven King**

## Elves

- **Elven Archer**
- **Elven Ranger**
- **Elven Herbalist**
- **Elven Scout:** really frail, with ridiculously low health and damage. Might be able to move across forests regardless of distance.

## Goblins

- **Goblin Rusher:** really good movement range.
- **Goblin Bruiser:** higher damage, maybe lower health gain.
- **Goblin Tank:** super high heal on attack, lower damage.
- **Goblin Mauler** _(name not settled)_: stops or slows down enemy movement, making the goblin mechanic more effective.
- **Goblin War Lord:** boosts goblins in some way, perhaps by increasing the rate of their health gain.

## Demons

- **Lord of Death:** sacrifices turn into zombies and boost the Lord of Death. Can raise corpses (skeletons?).
- **Legion:** sacrifices result in a split legion.
- **Cultist:** sacrificial unit.
- **Explosive Servant:** suicide unit. Maybe has to activate its suicide ability, which goes off in a few turns.
- **Zombie**

## Humans

- **Footman:** basic soldier.
- **Arbalist:** ranged option; takes a few turns to reload and fire again.
- **Defender:** high health/armor, low damage.
- **Alchemist:** weak; can heal, maybe other boosts (remove a stun or impairment?).
- **Worker:** clears land into plains. Time depends on the tile type, e.g. forest 3 turns, mountain 7 turns (just an idea).

## Wizards

- **Loyalist:** boosts wizards somehow.
- **Novice Wizard?** Does damage similar to the Grand Wizard, but damages itself.
- **Wizard Apprentice:** has some of the Grand Wizard's abilities, but doesn't do as much damage.
- **Grand Wizard:** super technical; can't stand prolonged up-close battles. Spell ideas:
  - Time
  - Fire
  - Lightning (damage in a straight line, piercing targets)
  - Ice
  - Earth
- **Battle Mage?** Can "get in the shiz" (as the kids say) and deal AoE damage.
