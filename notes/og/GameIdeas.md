# Game Ideas

## Starting Point

Basic framework to start: a simple grid, 1v1 human game. Other ideas can be added from there.

## Terrain

- Plains
- Dirt road
- Forest
- Hills
- Mountains
- Bridges
- Oceans
- Swamp
- Desert (maybe merfolk can't go on a desert tile?)

## Open Questions and Mechanics

- Should there be ground, air, and naval forces?
- Every race has its own "mage" unit with one or more abilities that tie into the race. For example:
  - The dwarf mage can change a mountain (create one where there wasn't one before, or change one that already exists).
  - The merfolk mage can change a tile into a water tile.
- Day/night cycle? Affects vision, maybe some abilities?
- Let some race play around with setting traps? Traps could do damage, or stop the affected unit from moving for a turn or two.
- Mercenaries on the map you can recruit? Getting them is really risky though.
- A unit that reveals hidden units/traps around it, but is pretty weak in a fight. Should all races get this? Just a few, or one? Or is it a mercenary unit?

## Races

### Elves

- Not slowed down by forests? Invisible in forests?
- Ranged attacks only? Poor defense against melee?
- Specialist unit that can limit a unit's movement if it lands a shot.

### Humans

- Maybe mechanically inclined? A well-rounded race?
- Mage healing ability? Or maybe a medic unit?
- Maybe some type of "light" magic abilities?

### Goblins

- Better movement through mountains than other races?
- Cheaper units = more units?
- Mage ability: put two basic goblins on the field.

### Dwarves

- Underground mechanic? Maybe some burrowing?
- Build defenses? Better at defensive play, but can play an offensive siege style?
- Mage ability: remove a mountain, or turn a mountain into a defensive structure or a tunnel only dwarves can use.

### Undead

- Come back to life after a certain number of turns?
- Maybe bring back enemy units?
- Maybe a unit that casts a spell on another unit, bringing it back to life if it dies while the spell is still in effect (lasts a certain number of turns). This creates an odd dynamic: an enemy might let the unit live for a while, and take some damage, because they don't want it to come back.

### Merfolk

- All units can move through water?
- Units may only exist out of water for a few turns? Or maybe they can only attack (or defend well) if they've touched water in the last _x_ turns?
- Mage who can summon water to a nearby location on the map.

### Demons?

- Mage ability: very low chance to mind control an enemy unit (permanent or temporary)?
- Really costly to produce; can reduce the cost with sacrifice?

## Mechanics to Explore

- Revealing fog of war.

## Game Modes

- **Deathmatch**
- **Helm's Deep:** defend a fortress for a certain number of turns. The defense gets fewer units than the offense, but maybe some sort of defensive bonus?
- **Capture the flag**
- **Protect the caravan**
- **Guard towns:** e.g. one big town and two little ones that each side protects. They give a bonus while they survive, maybe producing units every _x_ turns. Players might choose where to place them on the map, to try to lure the enemy into an ambush.
